Individual & Family Coverage FAQ

How do I know which health insurance plan is right for me or my family?

It comes down to your budget, the doctors and prescriptions you need covered, and the kind of care you and your family use most. I'll walk through your specific situation and compare real plans side by side, so you're choosing based on what actually fits — not just guessing.

What's the difference between marketplace and private health insurance?

Marketplace (ACA/Obamacare) plans are great for people who have higher healthcare needs or major medications. If your income is lower, you may also qualify for a tax credit that brings your premium down. Private plans, on the other hand, are typically underwritten based on your health history — so if you qualify, they can come with very strong rates and coverage. I'll help you figure out which route makes more sense for your situation.

Can I get insurance outside of open enrollment?

In most cases, yes. Open enrollment is the main window for marketplace plans, but qualifying life events — like losing a job, getting married, having a baby, or moving — open a special enrollment period. There are also private and short-term plan options available outside of open enrollment. Reach out and I'll let you know exactly what you qualify for.

How much does health insurance cost for individual or family coverage?

It varies quite a bit based on your age, location, household size, income, and the plan tier you choose. Rather than guessing, I'll pull real quotes for your specific situation so you can see actual numbers — including whether you qualify for any subsidies that lower the cost.
