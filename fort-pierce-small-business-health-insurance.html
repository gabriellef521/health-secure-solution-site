Small Business Health Insurance in Fort Pierce, FL

Small Business / Fort Pierce

By Gaby Fafard, Licensed Health Insurance Advisor · Fort Lauderdale, FL

If you own a business in Fort Pierce or anywhere in St. Lucie County, you've probably wondered whether offering health insurance is realistic for a team your size. It usually is, and it's often easier and more flexible than owners expect.

You Don't Need A Big Team

A lot of owners assume group coverage is only for companies with dozens of employees. It isn't. Group plans are available for teams as small as one or two people, and some plans are built specifically for owner-only businesses. If you've been holding off because you thought you were too small, it's worth a second look.

You're Not Required To Offer It, But It Helps

Generally speaking, smaller employers aren't required to offer health coverage. But "not required" and "not worth it" are different things. Offering a plan is one of the best ways a small business can compete with bigger companies for good people, especially in a market where employees are choosing between jobs.

How Much Do You Have To Cover?

There's no single required amount. Some plans ask the employer to contribute around half of the employee-only premium, and others are completely flexible and let you contribute what fits your budget. The same goes for how many employees need to sign up. Some plans require a minimum, and others work even if only a few people enroll. It comes down to which plan you choose, and that's where I can help.

Just Covering Yourself And Your Family?

If you're a business owner without employees yet, you still have options. There are plans designed for owner-only and very small businesses, so you can get coverage for yourself and your family without needing a full team in place first.
