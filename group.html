Common Questions

Group & Small Business Coverage FAQ

Am I required to offer health insurance as a small business owner?

No — it's not required, but offering group coverage helps you compete with larger companies and attract and retain higher-caliber employees.

How many employees do I need to qualify for group coverage?

It's often less than people think — some small business plans only require a single employee to qualify. I'll let you know exactly what you qualify for based on your team size.

How much of the premium am I required to cover as the employer?

That's up to you, but a common standard is covering around 50% of the employee's premium. I'll help you figure out what makes sense for your budget and team.

I'm a business owner just looking for coverage for myself and my family — can I still look at these plans?

Yes — even if you don't have employees yet, there are options built for owner-only or very small businesses. I'll help you find coverage that fits just you and your family.
